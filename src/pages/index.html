Real jobs, real skills, and a path to stability.

STEP is a paid, remote job built for people in Utah County who are working their way out of homelessness. It’s a first step toward steady, long-term employment.

Why STEP exists

The problem

No job without an address. No address without a job.

Employers expect references, reliable transportation, clean clothing, a permanent address, and a phone that works. For people experiencing homelessness, those requirements can close the door before an interview ever happens.

Where we come in

So we built a job that doesn’t ask for any of that.

None of that is required. And while participants rebuild their own lives, they help reconnect other families with their history.

How the job works

How STEP works

Three steps to a lasting job.

Here’s the path from day one to the next job.

  1. 1

    Start working

    • Paid from day one
    • 10 hours a week, on their own schedule
    • From a library, shelter, campus, or phone
  2. 2

    Build skills and stability

    • Weekly technology and workplace training
    • Hired as a part-time employee after a month
    • Steady pay toward housing, a phone, and transportation
  3. 3

    Move on to a lasting job

    • A recent, paid job to list on a resume
    • Help getting ID and other documents
    • Ready for stable, higher-paying work
